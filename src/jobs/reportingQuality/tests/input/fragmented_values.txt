## Climate Emissions 2023

### Scope 1
Direct emissions from our own operations: 5,200 tCO2e

### Scope 2 (market-based)
Reported per country of operation:
- Sweden: 1,240 tCO2e
- Germany: 860 tCO2e
- Poland: 430 tCO2e

### Scope 3
We report Scope 3 emissions using the GHG Protocol's 15 categories.

| Category | Description | tCO2e |
|---|---|---|
| Category 1 | Purchased goods and services | 22,000 |
| Category 6 | Business travel | 3,100 |
| Category 11 | Use of sold products | 145,000 |

We do not report the remaining categories as they are not material to our operations.

## Methodology

No changes to calculation methodology were made compared to 2022.
