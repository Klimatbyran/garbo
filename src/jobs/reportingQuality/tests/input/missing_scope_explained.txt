## Climate Emissions 2023

### Scope 1
Direct emissions from our own operations: 8,100 tCO2e

### Scope 2
Electricity and heating (market-based): 2,300 tCO2e

### Scope 3
We do not currently report Scope 3 emissions. We lack the supply chain
data required to produce a reliable estimate and plan to begin reporting
once our supplier engagement program is complete in 2025.

## Methodology

Emission factors for Scope 1 and Scope 2 were updated to the IEA 2023
dataset this year, restating our 2021 figures.
