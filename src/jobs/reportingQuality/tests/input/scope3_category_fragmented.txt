### Scope 3
We report Scope 3 emissions using the GHG Protocol's 15 categories.

Category 1 (Purchased goods and services) by material:
- Steel: 500 tCO2e
- Plastic: 200 tCO2e
- Aluminum: 90 tCO2e

Category 6 (Business travel): 3,100 tCO2e

Category 11 (Use of sold products) by product line:
- Line A: 60,000 tCO2e
- Line B: 40,000 tCO2e
- Total Category 11: 100,000 tCO2e

We do not report the remaining categories as they are not material to our operations.
